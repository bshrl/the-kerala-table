FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Common Questions

What is The Kerala Table?

The Kerala Table is a website created to showcase traditional Kerala dishes, flavours and food culture.

Where can I find the dishes?

Open the Food page from the navigation menu. There you can browse the dishes and use the search and category filters.

Can I search for a specific dish?

Yes. The Food page includes a search box that lets you quickly find dishes by name.

How do I return to the homepage?

Click HOME in the navigation menu or use the homepage button available throughout the site.
