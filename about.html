WELCOME TO TASTE OF KERALA

A Journey Through Kerala's Food Culture

Taste Of Kerala was created to celebrate the rich flavours, traditional dishes and food traditions of Kerala.

From simple everyday meals to grand festive celebrations, Kerala food brings together ingredients, family and tradition in memorable ways.

THE KERALA EXPERIENCE

Food Connected to Tradition

Kerala cuisine is shaped by its ingredients, climate, communities and long-standing cooking traditions.

Coconut, rice, spices, curry leaves and fresh local ingredients appear in many dishes, creating a wide range of flavours and textures.

Meals can be simple and comforting or colourful and celebratory, depending on the occasion.
